# Document pack for the build assignment

This is the document set for the Compliance Copilot exercise. 26 PDFs, about 85 pages.

## What's in here

The documents belong to a made-up financial services group called Northwind. There are policies, subordinate technical standards, operational procedures, and a staff handbook extract.

Worth knowing before you start:

- **The group has more than one legal entity.** Most documents belong to Northwind Financial Services Pvt. Ltd. Some belong to Northwind Capital Markets Ltd or Northwind Payments (Singapore) Pte Ltd. Those entity documents look very similar to the group ones but some of the numbers are different on purpose. Check the entity name in the header.
- **One document is superseded.** NFS-POL-001-A is an old version kept for audit reference. It is marked as superseded on the front page. It does not state current requirements.
- **One document is a summary, not a policy.** NFS-GUID-001 is a plain-language handbook extract. It says so itself, and it paraphrases the real policies loosely in at least one place.
- **The Records Retention Schedule (NFS-POL-011) is a governing document.** It explains in clause 1.2 how to resolve a retention period that appears differently in two places.
- Every document has numbered clauses (1.1, 4.3, and so on). Those make good citation targets.

None of this is accidental. Real policy corpora look like this, and handling it is most of the interesting work.

## Building your test set

Write your evaluation questions from these documents. Include:

- questions with a clear single answer,
- at least one question where two documents appear to disagree,
- at least a few questions the documents genuinely do not answer, so you can show what your system does when it has nothing to go on.

## Fine print

These are fictional documents written for a hiring exercise. They are not real policies, they describe no real company, and they are not legal or regulatory advice. You are free to use them however you like for this exercise.

If you would rather use your own document set, that's fine. Include it in your repo and mention it in your README.
