# Acceptable Use Policy

> **Sample document.** This is fictional content created for the Compliance Copilot demo project. It does not describe the policies of any real company.

**Document owner:** IT Department
**Effective date:** 2025-01-01
**Applies to:** All users of company-owned devices, networks, and accounts.

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes acceptable and prohibited use of company IT resources, including devices, networks, email, and cloud accounts.

## 2. General Use

Company IT resources are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited, reasonable personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties, consume excessive resources, or violate any other company policy.

## 3. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

- Install unapproved software or browser extensions on company devices without IT approval.
- Use company accounts or devices to access illegal content or engage in illegal activity.
- Share login credentials with anyone else, including colleagues.
- Connect personal storage devices (e.g., USB drives) to company laptops without an approved business reason.
- Use company email for large-scale personal solicitation, spam, or unauthorized commercial activity.
- Disable or attempt to bypass security controls such as EDR, disk encryption, or MFA.

## 4. Cloud Services and SaaS Tools

Employees may only use SaaS tools that have been approved through the IT procurement process. Uploading company or customer data to unapproved third-party tools (including personal AI/LLM accounts) is prohibited, as this data may leave the company's security perimeter.

## 5. Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Personal devices used to access company email or documents must have a passcode enabled and must enroll in the company's mobile device management (MDM) solution, which enables remote wipe of company data if the device is lost or stolen.

## 6. Remote Access

Remote access to internal systems must go through the company VPN or an approved zero-trust access solution. Public Wi-Fi should only be used with the VPN active.

## 7. Monitoring

The company reserves the right to monitor use of its IT resources, including email and network traffic, for security and compliance purposes, consistent with applicable law. Monitoring is not used to track personal use that falls within Section 2.

## 8. Enforcement

Violations of this policy may result in loss of system access, disciplinary action, or termination, depending on severity, and are handled under the process described in the Code of Conduct.
