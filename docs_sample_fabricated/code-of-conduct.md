# Code of Conduct

> **Sample document.** This is fictional content created for the Compliance Copilot demo project. It does not describe the policies of any real company.

**Document owner:** People Team
**Effective date:** 2025-01-01
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and interns.

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets expectations for professional behavior and describes how the company handles violations.

## 2. Core Principles

- Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect and professionalism.
- Act honestly in all business dealings and avoid conflicts of interest.
- Comply with all applicable laws and company policies.
- Protect company, customer, and colleague confidential information.

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

Employees must disclose any outside employment, financial interest, or personal relationship that could reasonably be seen to conflict with their duties, using the Conflict of Interest Disclosure Form. Disclosures are reviewed by the employee's manager and, where relevant, Legal.

## 4. Gifts and Hospitality

Employees may accept gifts or hospitality from vendors or partners only if the value is below 100 USD and the gift is not offered in connection with a pending decision (e.g., a procurement bid). Anything above this threshold must be declined or reported to a manager for review.

## 5. Anti-Harassment and Anti-Discrimination

The company prohibits harassment or discrimination based on race, gender, age, religion, disability, sexual orientation, or any other protected characteristic. Any employee who experiences or witnesses such conduct should report it through the process in Section 7.

## 6. Confidentiality

Employees must not disclose confidential company, customer, or colleague information to unauthorized parties, during employment or after departure, except as required by law.

## 7. Reporting Violations

Violations of this Code may be reported to a manager, People Team, or through the anonymous ethics hotline. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited.

## 8. Consequences of Violations

Confirmed violations of this Code may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment, depending on severity, and may be referred to law enforcement where applicable.

## 9. Annual Acknowledgment

All employees must review and electronically acknowledge this Code of Conduct annually as part of their compliance training.
