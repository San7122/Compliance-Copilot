# Data Retention Policy

> **Sample document.** This is fictional content created for the Compliance Copilot demo project. It does not describe the policies of any real company.

**Document owner:** Legal & Compliance
**Effective date:** 2025-01-01
**Applies to:** All employees, contractors, and systems that process company or customer data.

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how long different categories of data are retained by the company, and how data is disposed of once it is no longer needed. It exists to reduce legal risk, control storage costs, and satisfy regulatory obligations.

## 2. Retention Periods by Data Category

### 2.1 Customer Account Data

Customer account data (name, email, billing address, account settings) is retained for the duration of the customer relationship plus 3 years after account closure, to support billing disputes and legal claims.

### 2.2 Financial and Billing Records

Invoices, payment records, and tax-related documents are retained for 7 years from the date of transaction, in line with standard financial audit requirements.

### 2.3 Employee Records

Employee personnel files, including performance reviews and payroll records, are retained for 6 years after termination of employment.

### 2.4 Support Tickets and Communications

Customer support tickets and related email correspondence are retained for 2 years after ticket closure, after which they are automatically purged.

### 2.5 System and Access Logs

Server access logs, authentication logs, and audit trails are retained for 1 year. Security incident logs are retained separately for 5 years (see Incident Response Policy).

### 2.6 Marketing Data

Marketing lead data and campaign engagement records are retained for 18 months from the last recorded engagement, unless the individual has opted out sooner, in which case data is deleted within 30 days of the opt-out request.

## 3. Data Disposal

When a retention period expires, data must be disposed of using secure deletion methods:

- Digital records: permanent deletion from primary storage and backups within 90 days of the retention expiry date.
- Physical records: cross-cut shredding or incineration via an approved vendor.

## 4. Legal Holds

If data is subject to a litigation hold, active investigation, or regulatory request, normal retention schedules are suspended for that data until the hold is lifted, regardless of the timelines in Section 2.

## 5. Exceptions

Any exception to these retention periods must be approved in writing by the Data Protection Officer and documented in the exceptions register.
